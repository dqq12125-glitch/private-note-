回声岛 · 使用条款

适用于：回声岛公开版（App Store）· 版本 2026-11-20（草稿）

一句话说明：回声岛不登录也能玩；登录账号以后每天有免费的 AI 时间，买会员能多用。会员是 Apple 的自动续期订阅，到期前不取消就会续费，随时可以在 iPhone「设置 → 你的名字 → 订阅」里取消。

1. 谁提供回声岛

回声岛由 Echo of Forest Pty Ltd（下面叫「我们」）提供。你通过 App Store 下载使用，同时适用 Apple 的《授权应用最终用户许可协议》（标准 EULA）；这份条款是对它的补充，两者不一致时以对你更有利的为准。

2. 账号

  • 冒险、闯关、复习不用账号。和岛民聊天、口语自动打分等 AI 功能要登录（邮箱验证码，或者通过 Apple 登录）。
  • 账号只记邮箱（通过 Apple 登录可以隐藏邮箱）、年龄段、界面语言、能力级别、每天用了多少 AI、订阅状态。不存聊天记录和录音（举报的那几句除外，保留 90 天）。详见《隐私说明》。
  • 13 岁以下的孩子要家长先在邮件里同意，才能用语音聊天。
  • 你可以随时在「设置 → 账号和会员」里退出登录或删除账号；删除后服务器上的账号数据马上删掉，手机里的游戏进度不受影响。

3. 免费额度和会员

  • 免费账号每天有一定的 AI 时间（App 里「会员」页显示今天还剩多少、几点重置）。会员每天能用得更多。具体的额度以 App 里显示的为准，我们可能调整，调整前会在 App 里说明。
  • 会员有按月和按年两种，价格以 App Store 显示的为准（按你所在国家或地区的货币）。
  • 自动续期：确认购买时从你的 Apple 账户扣费。订阅会在每期结束时自动续期并扣费，除非你在当期结束前至少 24 小时关掉自动续期。
  • 怎么取消：iPhone「设置 → 你的名字 → 订阅」，或者 App 里「会员 → 管理订阅」。取消后当期剩下的时间照常能用。删除回声岛账号不会自动取消订阅。
  • 如果有免费试用，试用期没用完就买了订阅的，剩下的试用期作废。
  • 退款由 Apple 按它的规定处理（reportaproblem.apple.com）；退款后会员随即结束。

4. AI 生成的内容

  • 岛民的话、声音、评语和口语点评由人工智能生成，可能有错，只用来练习语言，不是专业意见。
  • 岛民只扮演游戏角色，不会问真名、学校、住址、电话。看到不合适的话，请按聊天里的「⚑ 举报」。

5. 使用规矩

  • 不要用回声岛做违法的事、骚扰别人，或者故意让 AI 说不合适的话；不要用自动程序刷免费额度、破解或转卖账号。
  • 违反这些规矩的，我们可以暂停或关闭账号（已付的订阅按 Apple 的规定处理）。

6. 其他

  • 服务可能因为维护、网络或 AI 供应商的原因暂时用不了；不登录的玩法不受影响。

For the public edition of Echo Island (App Store) · Version 2026-11-20 (draft)

In short: you can play Echo Island without an account. With an account you get some free AI time every day, and Membership gives you more. Membership is an Apple auto-renewable subscription: it renews until you cancel it, which you can do at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

1. Who provides Echo Island

Echo Island is provided by Echo of Forest Pty Ltd ("we"). Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement (standard EULA) applies; these terms add to it, and where they differ, the one more favourable to you applies.

2. Accounts

  • The adventure, levels and reviews work without an account. AI features (chatting with islanders, automatic speaking scores) need you to sign in with an email code or Sign in with Apple.
  • An account stores your email (Sign in with Apple can hide it), age group, interface language, level, how much AI you used each day and your subscription status. Chats and recordings are not stored, except the few lines you report, which are kept for 90 days. See the Privacy Notice.
  • Children under 13 need a parent to agree by email before they can use voice chat.
  • You can sign out or delete your account at any time in Settings → Account & Membership. Deleting removes your account data from our server immediately; the game progress on your device is not affected.

3. Free allowance and Membership

  • A free account gets some AI time every day (the Membership page shows what is left today and when it resets). Members get more each day. The app always shows the current allowance; we may change it and will tell you in the app first.

  • Auto-renewal: payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period and is charged again unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • How to cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or Membership → Manage subscription in the app. After cancelling you keep Membership until the end of the current period. Deleting your Echo Island account does not cancel the subscription.
  • If a free trial is offered, any unused part of it ends when you buy a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its rules (reportaproblem.apple.com); Membership ends when a refund is given.

4. AI-generated content

  • The islanders' words and voices, the chat comments and speaking tips are generated by AI. They can be wrong and are only for language practice, not professional advice.
  • Islanders only play game characters and never ask for a real name, school, address or phone number. If you see something inappropriate, tap "⚑ Report" in the chat.
